Redundant Safety Network

Offline Emergency Mesh Architecture

Tri-Channel Warning Redundancy: Whenever a disaster is verified by live AI cameras or reported by citizens, विपद्Sathi dispatches through 3 isolated channels simultaneously:

1. Telegram Alerts Instant photo snapshots and incident alerts to designated emergency dispatch bots and responder group channels.
2. Gmail SMTP Alerts High-priority HTML evidence reports with full camera snapshots delivered to authority mailboxes.
3. LoRa RF Offline Radio Long-range 433MHz emergency packets sent to offline community sirens and buzzer stations with zero internet dependency.

Hardware Firmware: The transmitter uses LORA/Sender/Sender.ino connected via USB Serial (9600 baud). Remote offline village nodes run LORA/Receiver/Receiver.ino with high-decibel piezo buzzers and strobe LEDs.
